# Personas and Glossary

## Personas
**Dad (product owner)**: technical but rusty. Runs the first big import and labels people.

**Mom**: non-technical. Imports her phone backup and searches for family photos.

**Kids (10 and up)**: use search and help name people. Need large buttons, plain words and no risky actions.

**Friend (Phase 3)**: installs the app for their own family; no technical help available.

## Glossary
- **Master library**: the app's own folder containing copies of all imported photos.
- **Source**: a folder photos were imported from.
- **Duplicate**: an exact copy (same hash) or near-identical photo (burst shots, edits). Flagged in Phase 1, reviewed and removed in Phase 2.
- **Screenshot**: an image captured from a screen, not taken with a camera.
- **Cluster**: a group of faces believed to be the same person.
- **Tracked person**: one of up to 15 people the family has named.
- **Unknown**: any face not assigned to a tracked person.
- **Suspect date**: an EXIF date that is probably wrong (scanner default, before 1990, in the future).
