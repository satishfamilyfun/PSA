# BA Standards and Definition of Ready

## User story format
As a [persona], I want [capability], so that [benefit]. Personas are listed on the Personas and Glossary page.

## Acceptance criteria format
Given / When / Then. Each criterion must be testable by QA without asking the author.

## Definition of Ready
A story is Ready for development when:
- It follows the user story format and names a persona.
- It has testable acceptance criteria, including error and edge cases.
- It matches its phase on the roadmap. Work belonging to another phase is moved to the right story, not silently dropped.
- All blocking tickets are Done, or the product owner has accepted the risk.
- Open questions in the ticket and in its blocking tickets are resolved.
- Attached designs (wireframes, mockups) match the description.

## Labels
- needs-clarification: open questions remain; not Ready.
- ready-for-dev: meets the Definition of Ready.
